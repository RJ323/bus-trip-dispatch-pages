Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

This page describes how the Trip Dispatch system collects and uses information, including for SMS (text message) notifications sent to drivers, riders, vehicle escorts, Trip Summary Roster recipients, and trip supervisors.

What we collect

Name and phone number for drivers, riders, vehicle escorts, Trip Summary Roster recipients, and trip supervisors, entered either by Authorized personnel into the dispatch roster or by the individual themselves through account sign-up. Trip details (date, time, pickup/dropoff location) are entered by personnel when a trip is created.

How we use it

Phone numbers are used solely to send SMS notifications related to trip assignments — new trip offers, confirmations, and status updates — to drivers, riders, and vehicle escorts on the department's active rosters; fill/summary notices to Trip Summary Roster recipients; and escalation alerts to trip supervisors. Messages are dispatch-related only; this system does not send marketing or promotional messages.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on trip volume and is tied directly to dispatch activity, not a fixed schedule.

Opt-out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts, or HELP for assistance. Message and data rates may apply. Since this system is used to assign and coordinate work trips, anyone who opts out should notify a dispatcher directly so trips can still be assigned and coordinated through another channel.

Sharing

Phone numbers and trip data are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties, except the service providers that operate this system on the business's behalf (SMS delivery via Twilio, scheduling via Google Calendar, data storage via Google Sheets, workflow automation via n8n).

Data retention

Roster and trip records are retained in Trip Dispatch Google Sheet for as long as needed for dispatch operations recordkeeping, consistent with the business records-retention policy.
